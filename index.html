Services

Tires, brakes, and everything after

  • Tires & wheels

    New and used tires, custom wheels, mounting and balancing.

    Typically includes

    • Mount and computer balance
    • New valve stems or TPMS service kit
    • Torque to factory spec
    • Old tire disposal
    • Pressure set and road test

  • Brakes & safety

    Pads, rotors, calipers and full brake inspections.

    Typically includes

    • Pads and rotors, front or rear
    • Hardware, shims and clips
    • Caliper slides cleaned and lubricated
    • Brake fluid level and condition check
    • Road test and bed-in

  • Engine & performance

    Diagnostics through engine work, foreign and domestic.

    Typically includes

    • Diagnosis before any teardown
    • Gaskets, seals and hardware replaced
    • Fluids refilled to spec
    • Post-repair scan and road test

  • Oil & fluid service

    Conventional through full synthetic, filters and fluids.

    Typically includes

    • Oil and filter, spec grade
    • Drain plug gasket replaced
    • All fluids topped off
    • Tire pressures set
    • Used oil disposal

  • A/C & heating

    Recharge, leak diagnosis and component replacement.

    Typically includes

    • Performance test, vent temperature measured
    • Leak check with dye or electronic detector
    • System evacuated and recharged
    • Refrigerant oil and dryer as needed

  • Cooling system

    Radiators, water pumps, thermostats and hoses.

    Typically includes

    • Coolant drain and flush
    • System pressure and cap test
    • Thermostat, hoses and clamps as needed
    • Bleed and heat-cycle check

  • Transmission

    Service, diagnosis and replacement.

    Typically includes

    • Fluid and filter service
    • Pan gasket replaced
    • Scan for transmission codes
    • Level set at operating temperature

  • Suspension & steering

    Struts, shocks, control arms and alignments.

    Typically includes

    • Struts, shocks, control arms or tie rods
    • Mounts, bearings and boots
    • Torque to spec at ride height
    • Alignment recommendation after install

  • Exhaust & emissions

    Exhaust repair and emissions troubleshooting.

    Typically includes

    • Leak inspection, manifold to tip
    • Gaskets, clamps and hangers
    • Oxygen sensor testing
    • Emissions readiness verified

  • Electrical & starting

    Batteries, starters, alternators and wiring faults.

    Typically includes

    • Battery load and charging system test
    • Starter draw test
    • Terminals cleaned, cables checked
    • Alternator output under load

  • Diagnostics & inspection

    Check-engine scans and pre-trip inspections.

    Typically includes

    • Full OBD scan, stored and pending codes
    • Live data under load
    • Visual inspection of the affected system
    • Written findings before work is quoted

  • Trucks & off-road

    Lift kits, oversized tires and truck-specific work.

    Typically includes

    • Lift kits and leveling components
    • Oversized tire fitment confirmed
    • Torque to spec at ride height
    • Alignment recommendation after install
